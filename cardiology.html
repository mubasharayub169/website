When to Consult Our Cardiologist in Gujrat

Visit Ikram Hospital's cardiology department on Bhimber Road if you experience:

Chest pain or discomfort
Shortness of breath
Irregular heartbeat or palpitations
High blood pressure
Family history of heart disease
Dizziness or fainting
Swelling in legs or ankles
Fatigue or weakness

Cardiology FAQs

What heart tests are available at Ikram Hospital Gujrat?

We offer comprehensive cardiac testing including ECG, 2D Echocardiography, Treadmill Test (TMT), Holter monitoring, and cardiac biomarker testing. All tests are performed by experienced technicians with modern equipment.

Is cardiology consultation available 24/7?

Emergency cardiac care is available 24/7 at our emergency department. For routine consultations, please book an appointment by calling (053) 3605377-78-79 or visit during OPD hours.

How can I prevent heart disease?

Heart disease prevention includes maintaining a healthy diet, regular exercise, avoiding smoking, controlling blood pressure and cholesterol, managing stress, and regular health checkups. Our cardiologists provide personalized prevention plans.
