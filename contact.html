Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to book a specialist appointment?

Call our appointments line for the fastest coordination. Our team helps with department selection and available consultant timings.

Can I visit directly without prior booking?

Yes, walk-ins are accepted based on OPD flow and emergency priority. Calling in advance helps reduce waiting time.

Do you support patient referrals from other clinics?

Yes. You can share referral details with our team and we will coordinate specialist review and follow-up guidance.
