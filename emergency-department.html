When to Visit Our Emergency Department in Gujrat

Call (053) 3605377-78-79 or visit immediately if you experience:

Life-Threatening

  • • Severe chest pain
  • • Difficulty breathing
  • • Unconsciousness
  • • Heavy bleeding
  • • Severe head injury

Urgent Care

  • • High fever (>103°F)
  • • Severe pain
  • • Sudden weakness
  • • Severe vomiting
  • • Allergic reactions

Special Cases

  • • Snake/animal bites
  • • Burns & scalds
  • • Poisoning
  • • Accidents
  • • Labor pains
